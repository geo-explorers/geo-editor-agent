# CLAIMS.md — claim semantics
## Practical use
### Record a claim judgment
Write each proposition with its timeframe, conditions and uncertainty intact. State the relationship being considered, then explain the exact inferential connection or difference using cited passages. Shared subject matter alone is insufficient. Record the decision, counterexample or unresolved ambiguity before proposing a relation.
Identify subject, predicate, modality, polarity, qualifiers, timeframe, conditions and comparator. A change from may to will changes the proposition. Preserve uncertainty and attribution.
Shared topic does not establish duplicate meaning, support or opposition. Test equivalence by checking whether either claim introduces an additional commitment. Test opposition under comparable scope and conditions. A supporting argument must provide a reason for the target proposition.
For claim grouping, follow the installed two-step process: adjudicate and establish Related claims; then assign only the exact bracket relationships justified by the rubric. Retain rejected and ambiguous pairs with reasons.
Debate mapping connects the question, positions, participants, arguments and evidence. Proposed wording and relations stay identifiable as proposals until reviewed and authorized.

## Curating claims for a debate product
Read Debate claim curation — working procedure before selecting or arranging claims. It is the canonical procedure for practical decisions in current discourse, broad-to-specific order, coherent collections, proposal novelty and feedback closure. Important missing positions and better wording alternatives belong inline, marked PROPOSED, with their closest existing claims linked beside them; the mirror roster must not limit the debate agenda. Topical relevance and correct links are insufficient acceptance criteria. One claim already permits agreement and disagreement; do not manufacture its opposite to fill a side.
